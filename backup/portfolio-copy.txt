TITLE: Frendigo App
SLUG: frendigo-app
STATUS: published
CATEGORIES: web-design, web-development, angular, graphics, frendigo, mobile-app-development, flutter, marketing, branding

EXCERPT
A three-platform ride marketplace built to replace surge with subscription pricing and direct pay.

CONTENT
I am the senior lead developer on Frendigo, a three-platform transportation marketplace I architected so riders and drivers are not trapped in someone else’s surge math. Clients plan commutes, pick providers from a live marketplace, see itemized estimates before they book, and pay the provider directly. Providers set their own rates, vehicles, and add-ons — and keep more of what they earn.

I built the mobile clients in Flutter for iOS and Android, the web experience in Angular, and the realtime layer on Firebase with Google Maps for mid-trip tracking. Pricing is a subscription engine fed by MPG, USDOT, and EIA gas data rather than a per-trip commission. Reservations can be split across providers, modified after booking, and extended with backtrack when something is left behind.

The product is in TestFlight and Android beta: a full commute OS, not a skin on a rideshare clone.

FEATURES
Client commute planner and marketplace, Provider rates, vehicles, and add-ons, Live maps and trip tracking, Itemized summaries and direct pay, iOS TestFlight and Android beta

CREATED WITH
Flutter (iOS / Android), Angular, Firebase, Google Maps API
